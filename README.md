# SalaryCalc.me - Free Salary Calculator Tools

A collection of free, fast, and accurate salary calculation tools running at [https://salarycalc.me](https://salarycalc.me).

## Features

- **Hourly to Salary Converter** - Convert hourly wage to annual salary instantly
- **Overtime Pay Calculator** - Calculate overtime earnings with different rate multipliers
- **Bonus & Commission Tax Calculator** - Understand how bonuses and commissions are taxed
- **Pay Raise Calculator** - See how a raise affects your take-home pay
- **Salary vs Hourly Comparison** - Compare the pros and cons of salary vs hourly employment
- **And more tools** - Tip Calculator, BMI Calculator, Loan Calculator, and more

## Why SalaryCalc.me?

Most salary calculators online are outdated or cluttered with ads. SalaryCalc.me provides clean, ad-light, accurate calculations with educational content to help you understand your paycheck better.
